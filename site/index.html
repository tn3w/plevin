What a lookup is, and is not


      

        

          Does the address I type leave the tab?
          
The lookup does not: the file is downloaded once and every answer is read out
            of it here. Three things do call out - api.ipify.org behind
            My address, cloudflare-dns.com and
            dns.google for the DNS card, and
            server.arcgisonline.com for the map tiles. The
            libraries ask none of those unless a dns flag says so.

        

        

          What does a lookup return?
          
98 fields: city, region, country, coordinates, postal code and timezone;
            ASN, operator, announcement, RPKI state and abuse mailbox; and abuse records
            with an address risk and a network risk score.

        

        

          Where does the data come from, and how often?
          
Rebuilt daily from MaxMind GeoLite2, IP2Location LITE, GeoNames, Natural
            Earth, a RIPE RIS RIB, RPKI ROAs, the NRO delegations, CAIDA, PeeringDB and
            156 abuse feeds.

        

        

          What can I type into the box?
          
Any IPv4 or IPv6 address, an ASN like AS13335, or a network
            name. A hostname is resolved over DNS first, then looked up like any other
            address.

        

        

          How exact is the location?
          
City level at best, and every place carries its own confidence and radius in
            kilometres. An IP address names a network, not a person or a street, so treat
            the coordinates as the centre of an area.
